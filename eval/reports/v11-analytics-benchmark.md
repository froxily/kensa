# Analytics load benchmark (v1.1)

> Generated by `scripts/benchmark_analytics.py` on SYNTHETIC rows in a dedicated local database (`kensa_bench_analytics`). Real measurements on this machine; they show how the aggregate and the dashboard scale, not anything about real CI data.
